# The user's rough walkthrough (as given)

Audience: business users. Length: 2–3 minutes. Recorded as a regular user.

```
Hello

First go to this url page "solvay-sparkai.ivolve.cloud"

You will see a sign in page, input your username and password, then click submit

Once you are logged in, you will be greeted in the main page

At the top you will two tabs "Spine" and "Fit-Gap Copilot"

Let's talk a look the "Spine" tab. Here you can see the knowledge graph that was built from the documents shared by the solvay team. The graphs how each business stream, core system and BPML processes are interconnected and their relatioships with each other. Feel free to explore the additional tabs like "Process", 'Model', "Cypher" and "Quality"

Next we will move to the "Fit-Gap Copilot" tab. Here you can see a source section, where you
can upload a document in PDF, Word or a text format. The uploaded document will be compared against
the solvay's gloabal template and SAP Best practices. In the scope section you also add some additional instructions for a specific area you like to focus on.

Once the document is uploaded, our pipeline will extract the contents from the documents

Once you are ready, then click the "Run Analysis" button. Right away you can see the agent start working on the request and you can view the logs in the screen.

The analysis will take approximately around 10 mins to complete, After the run is completed you can view the results
```
